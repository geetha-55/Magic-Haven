Our Menu

Paneer Butter Masala

A rich and creamy delight, our Paneer Butter Masala features soft paneer cubes simmered in a luscious tomato-based gravy, enriched with butter and aromatic spices.

Chicken Biryani

Experience the rich and aromatic flavors of our Chicken Biryani, a classic dish made with tender marinated chicken, basmati rice, and a blend of spices that create a symphony of taste.

Fish Fry

Savor the crispy perfection of our Fish Fry, featuring fresh, marinated fish fillets coated in a spiced batter and fried to golden brown. Each bite offers a delightful crunch followed by the tender, flaky fish inside.

Prawns Curry

Indulge in the rich flavors of our Prawns Curry, featuring succulent prawns simmered in a velvety coconut milk base infused with aromatic spices.

Gulab Jamun

Treat yourself to our exquisite Gulab Jamun, a classic Indian dessert made from soft, golden-brown milk dumplings soaked in a fragrant rose-infused syrup.

About Us

Welcome to Magic Haver, where culinary passion meets authentic flavors. Established in 2002, our mission is to bring you the finest dining experience with dishes crafted from the freshest ingredients. Our menu celebrates a variety of cuisines, offering something for everyone—from traditional favorites to contemporary creations.

At Magic Haver, we believe in creating not just meals, but memorable experiences. Join us for a delightful journey through flavors and enjoy our warm hospitality. Whether it’s a special occasion or a casual meal, we are here to serve you with love and dedication.
